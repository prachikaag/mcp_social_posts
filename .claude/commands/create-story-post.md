# Skill: Create Story Post (Everyday Woman Comic)

Create a single storytelling comic post for Miraya's "Woh Har Roz Ki Ladki" series.
Shows one everyday woman — her real life, her quiet strength — and how Miraya gives her
a moment that is entirely hers.

**Always reads `art_style.md` before generating any visual or prompt.**
**Always outputs as DRAFT. Never publishes without explicit user confirmation.**

---

## Before You Start

Read these files fully:
1. `CLAUDE.md` — brand voice, audience, CTA rules, products
2. `art_style.md` — character archetypes, colour palettes, panel structure, base image prompt

---

## Inputs

- **Story theme** — what moment of her life does this post capture?
  If not provided, choose from: *morning routine / work + home juggle /
  festival preparation / a rare quiet moment / dressing up for someone she loves*
- **Character archetype** — read from `art_style.md` (A/B/C/D), or ask user
- **Colour palette** — read from `art_style.md`, default to Palette 4 (Monsoon Soft) for
  everyday stories, Palette 1 (Coral Dawn) for collection launches
- **Number of panels** — default: 5 panels as carousel (one emotion per slide)

---

## Step-by-Step

### Step 1 — Read art_style.md
Note:
- The base image generation prompt
- The emotional arc (reality → small moment → Miraya → glow)
- The chosen colour palette hex codes
- The character archetype for this story

### Step 2 — Write the Story Arc
Map the 5 panels:

| Panel | Emotion | Scene |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Her reality — she carries so much | [specific everyday scene from her life] |
| 2 | She keeps going — quietly, without complaint | [second scene showing her strength] |
| 3 | A tiny crack — she barely has time for herself | [the moment she almost forgets herself] |
| 4 | She puts on her Miraya piece | [the turning point — she chooses herself] |
| 5 | She glows — not changed, just remembered | [she is radiant, whole, seen] |

### Step 3 — Write Panel Narration Text
For each panel, write:
- **Narration box text** (max 10 words, Hinglish)
- **Speech bubble** (if any — max 8 words, natural Hinglish)
- **Emotion caption** between panels (1 line, serif font)

### Step 4 — Generate Image Prompts
For each panel, write a complete image generation prompt using the BASE PROMPT
from `art_style.md` + specific scene description.

Format:
```
PANEL [N] IMAGE PROMPT:
[Base prompt from art_style.md]
Scene: [specific description]
Character: [archetype, expression, posture, what she's wearing]
Setting: [location, time of day, background elements]
Colour palette: [palette name] — Background: [hex], Primary: [hex], Accent: [hex]
Text overlay: "[narration box text]" — [font style] in [text colour hex]
Emotion: [the one word that captures this panel]
```

### Step 5 — Write the Caption
Follow the caption structure from `art_style.md`:
- Line 1: resonant question or observation (Hinglish)
- Lines 2–4: her story in short Hinglish lines
- Line 5: the turn — she chooses herself
- Final line: the brand emotional statement
- CTA: DM or Planet Mall only (no website links)

### Step 6 — Assemble the Draft
