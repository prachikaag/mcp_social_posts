# Skill: Understand Why It's Trending

Go deeper than "what is trending" — analyse the underlying reason a keyword,
format, or topic is gaining traction right now. This informs which emotional
or cultural angle your campaign should take.

---

## When to Use This Skill

Run this after `/fetch-trending-posts` when you want to understand the "why"
behind a trend before building a campaign. Without understanding the reason,
campaigns can feel forced or miss the emotion that made something go viral.

---

## Inputs Required

- **Keyword or trend to analyse** (from a previous fetch-trending-posts run, or provided directly)
- **Brand context** — read from CLAUDE.md

---

## Steps

1. **Identify the trigger** — what started this trend?
   - Is it tied to a festival, season, or cultural moment? (Diwali, monsoon, back-to-school)
   - Is it a reaction to a news event, viral meme, or celebrity moment?
   - Is it an evergreen topic that resurfaces cyclically?
   - Is it driven by a platform algorithm push (e.g., Instagram promoting a new format)?

2. **Identify the core emotion** — what feeling is driving engagement?
   Search for: `why is [keyword] trending`, `[keyword] viral reason India`
   - Pride / identity (e.g., "Make in India", regional pride)
   - Nostalgia (e.g., old Bollywood references, childhood memories)
   - Aspiration (e.g., luxury look for less, glow-up)
   - Humour / relatability (e.g., meme format, "this is so me")
   - Urgency / FOMO (e.g., limited time, sold out everywhere)
   - Community / belonging (e.g., "all Indian women know this feeling")

3. **Identify who is leading it** — who started or amplified this trend?
   - Regular users / UGC
   - Influencers / micro-creators
   - Big brands (and if so, is there room for smaller brands?)
   - News or media coverage

4. **Assess trend lifecycle** — where is this trend right now?
   - **Early (rising):** Get in now for maximum reach
   - **Peak:** High competition, hard to stand out — only join if you have a unique angle
   - **Declining:** Avoid unless you have a clear subversive or ironic angle

5. **Find the brand-relevant hook** — given your product and audience (CLAUDE.md),
   what specific angle on this trend fits your brand authentically?

---

## Output Format

### Trend Analysis: [Keyword/Topic]
**Date:** [today's date]

#### What Triggered This Trend
[2-3 sentences explaining the origin or trigger]

#### Core Emotion Driving It
**Primary emotion:** [e.g., Nostalgia]
**Why it resonates with Indian audiences:** [brief explanation]

#### Who Is Leading It
[Creators, brands, or communities amplifying this trend]

#### Trend Lifecycle Stage
- [ ] Early / Rising
- [ ] Peak
- [ ] Declining

**Recommendation:** [join now / join with unique angle / avoid]

#### Why It Works (The Real Reason)
<!-- The deeper cultural or psychological insight — this is the most important part -->
[e.g., "This trend is working because it validates something Indian women feel but rarely
see acknowledged — the pressure to look 'put together' on a budget. Brands that speak
to that truth will get saved and shared, not just liked."]

#### Best Angle for [Your Brand Name]
<!-- Tailored to CLAUDE.md brand context -->
[e.g., "Lead with the 'affordable doesn't mean basic' angle. Show that your kurtas
deliver the aspirational look without the aspirational price tag."]

#### What to Avoid
[e.g., "Don't just slap a trending audio on a product photo — this audience is smart
enough to spot lazy content. The trend is working because of the storytelling, not the audio."]
